## 🌙 About the Game

**Ghosty Garden** is a Halloween-themed farming simulation game where players step into the role of a mystical gardener. In this haunted world, your primary goal is to complete various missions by growing and harvesting specialized crops to satisfy the demands of ghostly customers.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌱 **Farming Simulation** — Plant, grow, and harvest Halloween-themed crops
- 👻 **Ghostly Customers** — Fulfill unique orders from spooky characters
- 📋 **Mission System** — Complete missions to progress through the game
- 🎃 **Halloween Atmosphere** — Immersive haunted world aesthetic
- 🌐 **Play in Browser** — No installation required, runs on WebGL

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open the game link in your browser
2. Choose crops to plant based on customer demands
3. Water and tend to your garden until crops are ready to harvest
4. Deliver the harvested crops to satisfy your ghostly customers
5. Complete all missions to win!
